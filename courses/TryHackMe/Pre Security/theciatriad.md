The CIA Triad is made up of three core principles of cybersecurity:
Confidentiality — Protecting information from unauthorized access or disclosure.
Integrity — Ensuring that data is not modified or changed without authorization.
Availability — Ensuring that systems and information remain accessible when users need them.

When an incident occurs, security professionals can ask questions such as:
- Was sensitive data exposed to unauthorized individuals?
- Was data modified without permission?
- Were systems or services unavailable when users needed them?

Unauthorized access → Confidentiality
Unauthorized modification → Integrity
Service outage → Availability
